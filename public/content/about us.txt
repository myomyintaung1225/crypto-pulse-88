## About PrimeBlock

PrimeBlock is a digital asset platform built for clarity and control. We combine live market data with a focused trading workflow across crypto, metals, and forex.

## What we stand for

Transparency in pricing, disciplined risk messaging, and responsive support are central to how we operate. We invest in stable infrastructure so you can concentrate on decisions—not downtime.

## Getting started

Browse markets from the home screen, review asset details, and use the trade flow when you are ready. If you need help, reach customer service through the channels shown in the app.
