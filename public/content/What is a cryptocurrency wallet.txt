## What is a cryptocurrency wallet?

A wallet holds the keys that authorize transfers on a blockchain. The wallet does not “store coins” off-chain in the way a bank stores deposits—balances live on the ledger.

## Custodial vs non-custodial

Custodial wallets manage keys on your behalf with recovery flows you configure. Non-custodial wallets put key custody entirely on you.

## Backups

Write down recovery phrases offline. Anyone with the phrase can move funds—treat it like cash.

## Security habits

- Prefer hardware signing for large balances when appropriate
- Double-check addresses and networks before sending
