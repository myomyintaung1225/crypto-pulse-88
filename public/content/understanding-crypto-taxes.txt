## Understanding crypto taxes

Tax treatment depends on your country, residency, and facts of each transaction. The notes below are general information—not tax advice.

## Common taxable events

- Selling crypto for fiat
- Trading one crypto for another
- Using crypto to pay for goods or services (in many regimes)

## Record keeping

Keep timestamps, amounts, counterparty labels, and fees. Consistent records reduce reconciliation effort at year-end.

## Professional help

Consult a qualified tax professional who understands digital assets in your jurisdiction.
