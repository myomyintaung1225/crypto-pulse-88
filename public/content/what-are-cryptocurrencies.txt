## What are cryptocurrencies?

Cryptocurrencies are digital assets that typically rely on cryptography and distributed ledgers to record transfers without a single central issuer.

## Why people use them

- Programmable settlement on public networks
- 24/7 market access for many instruments
- Alternative rails for cross-border value movement

## Risks to understand

Prices can be volatile. Protocol upgrades, bridge vulnerabilities, and phishing remain common threats—verify sources and protect keys.
