## Forex vs crypto trading

Forex focuses on fiat currency pairs with established macro drivers. Crypto markets often move faster and can trade across more sessions with distinct liquidity profiles.

## Liquidity and spreads

Major forex pairs can be deep during institutional sessions. Crypto liquidity varies widely by token and venue.

## Volatility

Crypto may exhibit sharper short-term swings; forex can still gap around news events.

## Which fits you?

Choose based on your risk tolerance, time horizon, and whether you prefer standardized fiat macro narratives or token-specific catalysts.
